♨️ Jiaoxi (Nights 3, 4, 5)

Hotel PIN Jiaoxi

Address: No. 2, Lane 21, Jiankang 2nd St., Jiaoxi Township, Yilan County ｜ About 0.5 km from Jiaoxi Train Station. The hotel offers a free shuttle (advance phone booking required the day before), or a taxi ride of about 5 minutes.

In-room private hot spring tub Outdoor hot spring SPA Kids' splash pool / play room Free afternoon tea & late-night snacks Rating 8.7-8.9
⚠️ Note

Jiaoxi and Yilan are neighboring stations on the TRA line (Jiaoxi comes first, then Yilan) — the train ride from Taipei to either is roughly the same (about 60-80 minutes). However, most attractions (Momoyama Park, National Center for Traditional Arts, Meihua Lake, Luodong Night Market) are actually in the Yilan/Luodong area, meaning an extra 15-30 minutes by taxi from Jiaoxi. Travel times and estimated fares for each day are noted in detail below.

① Theme: Arrive early morning on the red-eye flight, drop off luggage first, explore the neighborhood around the hotel to ease into the trip — no far-flung sightseeing today

  • Qsquare → Taipei Underground Mall: directly connected underground, no need to exit

④ Tips for Kids & Grandparents

Helpful Tips

  • Luggage can be left at the front desk before check-in time — no need to drag suitcases around while sightseeing.
  • Both the Underground Mall and Qsquare are indoor, air-conditioned spaces — ideal for adjusting to jet lag and avoiding the sun.

⑤ Rainy-Day Alternative

Rain Plan
  • Today's plan is already centered on indoor malls and the underground mall, so it's essentially unaffected by weather — no adjustments needed.

Day 2｜Oct 26 (Mon) Taipei

① Theme: Dadaocheng Old Street in the morning, Ximending / Carrefour shopping in the evening

② Detailed Timeline

TimeActivity
9:30 AM-12:00 PMDadaocheng / Dihua Street — dried goods stores, fabric shops, historic architecture, all flat terrain, great for a leisurely stroll with grandparents; can also visit Xiahai City God Temple nearby
NoonLunch near Dihua Street (traditional Taiwanese snacks and dried-goods eateries, plenty of seating)
2:00-5:00 PMXimending — Red House (historic building with cafés/shops on the ground floor for a break), Rainbow Square for photos, shopping; skip long-queue famous eateries and choose restaurants with seating instead
5:00-7:00 PMCarrefour (Ximen or Guilin branch) for snacks, daily necessities, and souvenirs — a 24-hour supermarket locals love too
EveningDinner near Ximending (recommended: San Wei Eatery, Wan Guo Marinated Foods — no long waits)

③ Transport Guide

  • Hotel → Dadaocheng: MRT or a short walk + taxi, about 15-20 minutes
  • Dadaocheng → Ximending: MRT Bannan/Songshan-Xindian Line with a transfer, about 15-20 minutes
  • Ximending → Hotel: 1 MRT stop back to Taipei Main Station, or about a 20-minute walk

④ Tips for Kids & Grandparents

Helpful Tips
  • Both Dadaocheng and Ximending are mostly flat, with several rest spots along the way (tea houses, Eslite Bookstore).
  • Ximending gets crowded on weekends/Mondays — try to avoid the 2-6 PM peak, or split the family into groups for shopping.

⑤ Rainy-Day Alternative

Rain Plan
  • Dadaocheng can be swapped for the indoor Xin Fang Chun Tea House or browsing fabric shops in Yongle Market (indoor spaces).
  • Ximending has plenty of indoor malls (Eslite Ximen, Don Don Donki, Wannian Building) — rain won't affect the plan at all.

Day 3｜Oct 27 (Tue) Taipei → Jiaoxi

① Theme: Train south to Jiaoxi, an easy photo-op destination in the afternoon, first hot spring soak in the evening

② Detailed Timeline

TimeActivity
9:00-10:00 AMCheck out of the Taipei hotel, head to Taipei Main Station
10:00 AM-12:00 PMTake the TRA train to Jiaoxi (journey ~60-80 minutes)
NoonArrive at Jiaoxi Station, take Hotel PIN Jiaoxi's free shuttle (or a ~5-minute taxi ride) to check in; lunch in the Jiaoxi area (recommended: Sanxing scallion pancakes, Yilan gao zha, and pork dishes — skip beef options)
2:00-4:00 PMMomoyama (Yilan) Park — about a 15-20 minute taxi ride from Jiaoxi, next to Yilan Station; art installations perfect for photos, the grounds are flat with no slopes
EveningTaxi back to Jiaoxi (~15-20 min), dinner in the Jiaoxi street area (many hot-spring eateries and souvenir shops within walking distance), then back to the hotel for a relaxing soak and early rest

③ Transport Guide

  • Taipei Main Station → Jiaoxi Station: TRA Tze-Chiang/Puyuma/Taroko Express ~60-80 min, fare approx. NT$277 (local trains ~NT$179 but take longer — express trains are more comfortable)
  • Jiaoxi has no HSR — only TRA trains or Kamalan buses (board at Banqiao Station / Taipei Bus Station / City Hall Bus Station, journey ~80-110 min, fare NT$140-150)
  • Jiaoxi Station → Hotel PIN Jiaoxi: free shuttle provided (book by phone the day before) or ~5-minute taxi ride
  • Jiaoxi → Yilan City (Momoyama Park, etc.): ~15-20 minute taxi ride, fare approx. NT$250-300
  • Book TRA reserved-seat tickets in advance via the website/app (up to 28 days ahead) to secure a seat — weekday trips are easier to book preferred trains

④ Tips for Kids & Grandparents

Helpful Tips
  • Reserved-seat TRA trains have luggage storage areas — book aisle seats in advance so grandparents can move around more easily.
  • Momoyama Park is free and doesn't require much standing — a great gentle warm-up after arriving in Yilan. If anyone is tired, it's fine to simply stay at the hotel to soak and rest, then head out for dinner in the evening.
  • Every room at Hotel PIN Jiaoxi has its own hot spring tub — you can soak right after arriving to ease the fatigue of travel. Free welcome snacks are also available in the lobby from the afternoon onward, another nice way to unwind.

⑤ Rainy-Day Alternative

Rain Plan
  • Some installations at Momoyama Park have covered walkways, so light rain won't be much of an issue. In heavier rain, switch to the Yilan Distillery (indoor, tastings & tours) or the Kavalan Sweets Factory (indoor DIY experience), or simply stay indoors at the hotel to soak and use the play room — no weather impact.

Day 4｜Oct 28 (Wed) Yilan

① Theme: National Center for Traditional Arts + Dongshan River Nature Park — flat, stroller/wheelchair-friendly grounds, ideal for three generations to enjoy together

② Detailed Timeline

TimeActivity
9:30 AM-12:30 PMNational Center for Traditional Arts (NCFTA) — traditional architecture district, hands-on DIY experiences, puppet-show performances; the entire area has flat pathways and free wheelchair rental, easy on grandparents
NoonLunch at the NCFTA food court (wide variety, including kids' meals)
2:00-4:00 PMDongshan River Nature Park — adjacent to NCFTA, stroll along the waterfront; no extra transport needed, a relaxed pace
EveningTaxi back to Jiaoxi (~30-35 min), dinner in the Jiaoxi area (recommended: Zhenghao Fresh Meat Xiaolongbao, Yilan-style pork dishes), then back to the hotel to soak and rest

③ Transport Guide

  • Jiaoxi → NCFTA: best to book a taxi directly (~30-35 min, fare approx. NT$450-500) — quicker and easier than taking a shuttle bus
  • NCFTA → Dongshan River Nature Park: walk or use the park shuttle — the two are very close together

④ Tips for Kids & Grandparents

Helpful Tips
  • NCFTA has plenty of rest areas and handrails in the restrooms — widely considered one of the most senior-friendly attractions in Yilan.
  • Arrive around 9:30 AM to avoid crowds; finish the main sightseeing before noon and leave the afternoon for a relaxed stroll at the water park.

⑤ Rainy-Day Alternative

Rain Plan
  • Most of NCFTA's buildings have covered walkways — light rain has minimal impact.
  • In heavier rain, swap the afternoon plan for Ankang Lohas Museum (an indoor kid-friendly attraction with AR interactive exhibits, fully indoors).

Day 5｜Oct 29 (Thu) Yilan

① Theme: Meihua Lake + Luodong Night Market — Yilan's most senior-friendly lakeside walk, followed by an evening of local street food

② Detailed Timeline

TimeActivity
9:30-11:30 AMMeihua Lake Scenic Area — a ~4 km flat lakeside path with pavilions and benches along the way; stroll, cycle, or rent a pedal boat; widely regarded as the most senior-friendly attraction in Yilan
NoonLunch near Meihua Lake or back in town
2:00-4:00 PMEasy activity — Longtan Lake Park (also flat and free) or head back to the hotel for a nap, depending on everyone's energy
5:00-6:00 PMLuodong Night Market — arrive early to beat the 6:30-8:30 PM crowd; recommended: dragon-phoenix legs, bao xin fen yuan, and pork dishes; skip stalls with long queues
From 7:30 PMTaxi back to Jiaoxi (~20-25 min), then relax with a soak at the hotel

③ Transport Guide

  • Jiaoxi → Meihua Lake: best to book a taxi directly, ~25-30 min, fare approx. NT$400-450
  • Meihua Lake/Longtan Lake → Luodong Night Market: ~15-20 min by taxi; Luodong Night Market → Jiaoxi: ~20-25 min — arrange your evening taxi in advance or ask the hotel to help book one

④ Tips for Kids & Grandparents

Helpful Tips
  • Meihua Lake has little shade in autumn — bring hats for sun protection and a folding chair so grandparents can rest whenever needed.
  • Luodong Night Market can get crowded — try to go at off-peak times, and split up to grab food from different stalls to reduce wait times.

⑤ Rainy-Day Alternative

Rain Plan
  • Meihua Lake's path is largely uncovered — in heavy rain, switch to the indoor Yilan Museum of Art or the Zhongxing Cultural and Creative Park (indoor exhibits + market).
  • Luodong Night Market has mostly semi-open stalls, so light rain has little impact — bring umbrellas.

Day 6｜Oct 30 (Fri) Jiaoxi → Taipei

① Theme: Train back north, a relaxed stroll through old Taipei lanes in the afternoon, dinner at Sanchong Bawei Ginger Duck Hotpot in the evening

② Detailed Timeline

TimeActivity
9:00-10:00 AMCheck out of Hotel PIN Jiaoxi, take the hotel shuttle or a taxi (~5 min) to Jiaoxi Station
10:00 AM-12:00 PMTake the TRA train back to Taipei (journey ~60-80 minutes)
NoonArrive at Taipei Main Station, check into the hotel, lunch nearby
2:00-4:00 PMRevisit the Houzhan area to pick up anything missed — snacks, drugstore items, stationery; a relaxed, low-effort pace
4:30-5:00 PMTake the MRT to Sanchong (Taipei Main Station → transfer at Zhongxiao Xinsheng Station → Zhonghe-Xinlu Line to Sanchong/Cailiao Station, ~25-30 min); leave early to beat the evening dinner rush
5:30-7:00 PMBawei Ginger Duck Hotpot – Original Flagship Store — the whole family shares the signature charcoal-fired ginger duck hotpot and the "Bawei Trio" (meatballs, ling ling rolls, fresh tofu skin) for a warming meal; aim to be seated by 5:00 PM to avoid the 6:30-8:30 PM rush
7:30-8:30 PMTake the MRT back to the hotel near Taipei Main Station, early rest

③ Transport Guide

  • Jiaoxi Station → Taipei Main Station: TRA reserved-seat train ~60-80 min
  • The surrounding shopping area is walkable right from the station — no extra transport needed
  • Taipei Main Station → Bawei Ginger Duck Hotpot: take the Bannan Line 2 stops to Zhongxiao Xinsheng Station, transfer to the Zhonghe-Xinlu Line (toward Huilong) for about 5 stops to Sanchong Station or Cailiao Station, ~25-30 minutes total, then a 6-8 minute walk; address: No. 100, Sec. 1, Chongyang Rd., Sanchong Dist., New Taipei City

④ Tips for Kids & Grandparents

Helpful Tips
  • Today is designed as a "transition and recovery" day, deliberately kept light so everyone can recover from the busy days in Yilan.
  • If anyone is tired, feel free to rest at the hotel in the afternoon and head out to Sanchong in the evening instead.
  • Ginger duck hotpot is warming and rich — grandparents should eat in moderation and not overindulge; kids can stick to milder sides (like cabbage or noodles) to avoid an upset stomach before the late flight the next day.
  • The Bawei Ginger Duck Hotpot flagship store doesn't take reservations and is only open in autumn/winter (roughly October through spring) — check their Facebook page beforehand to confirm they're open that day.

⑤ Rainy-Day Alternative

Rain Plan
  • Taipei Main Station itself is a large indoor mall (underground mall + station concourse) — shopping and dining there is completely unaffected by rain.
  • Bawei Ginger Duck Hotpot has fully indoor seating (across three floors), so rain won't affect this part of the plan either.

  • Hotel → Taoyuan Airport: the Airport MRT entrance is right across from the hotel, direct express train ~35 min; allow more than 3 hours for a relaxed pace
  • Everything today stays within walking distance of Taipei Main Station — no long-distance transport needed, so you can return to the hotel to rest any time or head to the airport early if needed

④ Tips for Kids & Grandparents

Helpful Tips
  • The last day is deliberately kept within walking distance of Taipei Main Station, requiring no transportation at all — this avoids extra fatigue and ensures both grandparents and the child have enough energy for tonight's red-eye flight.
  • Since you'll be flying overnight, keep today's schedule light and unhurried — the afternoon rest period is important and shouldn't be skipped.
  • Pack large luggage items the night before so you only need to handle personal items on the day, minimizing last-minute rushing.
  • If anyone is tired, feel free to simply rest at the hotel in the afternoon — dinner can also be handled near the hotel if preferred.

⑤ Rainy-Day Alternative

Rain Plan
  • Nanmen Market is an indoor market, completely unaffected by weather.
  • Ningxia Night Market has mostly semi-open stalls — in heavier rain, switch to the Taipei Underground Mall or Qsquare for dining and shopping, both also within walking distance of the hotel.

4. Packing List & Practical Tips

✅ Documents & Finances

Essential Documents

  • Passport (valid for 6+ months) plus a photo backup saved on your phone
  • Digital copy of flight tickets / boarding passes
  • Hotel booking confirmations (in both English and Chinese, handy for showing to taxi drivers)
  • Travel insurance policy

Money & Connectivity

  • New Taiwan Dollar cash (some food stalls and markets don't accept cards)
  • EasyCard (available at the airport — usable for MRT, buses, and many shops)
  • Credit card (accepted at Nanmen Market, Carrefour, and chain restaurants)
  • An overseas data SIM or portable WiFi (buy at Changi or online in advance)

✅ For Grandparents & Kids

For Grandparents

  • Regular medications (blood pressure, digestive aids, etc.) — keep in carry-on, not checked luggage
  • Comfortable, non-slip walking shoes (this trip involves a lot of walking — good shoes matter more than luggage)
  • A warm jacket (useful for the early-morning arrival and after hot spring soaks; Taiwan mornings/evenings in October can be cool)
  • Reading glasses / spare hearing-aid batteries

For the Child

  • Regular children's medications (fever reducer, digestive aids, insect repellent)
  • A lightweight stroller (useful for longer walks at Meihua Lake, NCFTA, etc.)
  • Kid-friendly hot spring toys (Hotel PIN Jiaoxi has a private in-room hot spring tub)
  • A comfort toy/blanket (helpful during the red-eye flight and unfamiliar surroundings)

✅ Clothing & Miscellaneous

Clothing

  • A light raincoat/foldable umbrella (both Taipei and Yilan have a chance of rain in October)
  • Non-slip, water-resistant shoes (useful for lakeside paths and any ferry decks)
  • Swimwear for the hot spring (required in the outdoor SPA area at Hotel PIN Jiaoxi)
  • One warm jacket (for temperature swings and air-conditioned spaces)

Miscellaneous

  • Chargers, power bank, and plug adapter (Taiwan uses 110V — different from Singapore's sockets)
  • A small reusable shopping bag (handy for souvenir shopping and markets)
  • Wet wipes and hand sanitizer (useful before/after eating at night markets and traditional markets)
  • A reusable water bottle (many attractions have water refill stations)

✅ Insurance & Other Notes

Insurance & Other Notes
  • We recommend travel insurance that covers flight delays/cancellations, especially given the grandparents traveling and the possibility of late-season typhoon effects in October.
  • Since the return flight is a late-night red-eye on Oct 31, avoid over-scheduling that day and keep enough downtime for everyone.
  • Book TRA reserved-seat tickets between Jiaoxi and Taipei in advance via the website/app (up to 28 days ahead) — weekend trains are popular, so book early.
  • All meals in this itinerary avoid beef dishes (you can decide for yourself on beef noodle soup or mutton soup options), focusing instead on local Yilan/Taipei pork, seafood, and noodle dishes.
  • Bawei Ginger Duck Hotpot only operates in autumn/winter — check their Facebook page beforehand to confirm they're open on the day you plan to visit.

5. Trip Design Principles Recap

📋 Trip Design Principles Recap

  • Balanced pacing: One major attraction each morning, one or two lighter activities in the afternoon, ensuring both grandparents and the child get enough rest.
  • Simple transport: The whole trip relies on TRA trains, MRT, and taxis for short hops — no self-driving, no complicated multi-day transit passes.
  • Centralized lodging: Near Taipei Main Station · Roaders Plus Hotel (3 nights) + Jiaoxi · Hotel PIN Jiaoxi (3 nights) — only one hotel change for the whole trip.
  • Avoiding pitfalls: No long-queue trendy restaurants, no steep hiking-only trails, and skipping Huashan/Songshan Creative Parks, Taipei 101, Jiufen, Shifen, the National Palace Museum, and Yuanshan.
  • All requested spots included: Taipei Houzhan area, Qsquare, the Underground Mall, Dadaocheng, Ximending, Carrefour, Bawei Ginger Duck Hotpot, Nanmen Market, and Ningxia Night Market are all in the itinerary — with Day 1, Day 2, and Day 7 having zero overlap.
  • Red-eye flight friendly: The final day is entirely within walking distance of Taipei Main Station, with a dedicated afternoon rest period, and the hotel is right across from the Airport MRT entrance — no need to backtrack into the city that evening.
