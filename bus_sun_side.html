“Left / Right” means from the passenger’s view, facing the front of the bus. Sun position is calculated from your location and time; clouds, buildings, trees and road curves are not considered. Plan mode assumes a straight line from A to B. If the sun is almost overhead (around midday near the equator), the side matters little.
